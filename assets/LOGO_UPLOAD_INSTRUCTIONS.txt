Upload your logo images here with these exact filenames:

1. icon.png
   - App icon.
   - Recommended size: 1024 x 1024 px.
   - PNG, square, no transparency if possible.

2. adaptive-icon.png
   - Android adaptive icon foreground.
   - Recommended size: 1024 x 1024 px.
   - Keep the logo centered with padding around it.

3. splash.png
   - Splash screen logo.
   - Recommended size: 1242 x 2436 px, or a centered logo on transparent/solid background.

After uploading these files to the assets folder, rebuild the app once in EAS.
